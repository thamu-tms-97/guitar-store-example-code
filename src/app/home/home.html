Devin's Guitar Store: A Tribute to Devin Landes

Dear Students,

Devin Landes is one of my former students who I miss very much. I'm still in touch with his family and they wrote this message to each of you.

Message from the Landes Family

Our family is so grateful for this tribute to our beautiful son and brother Devin. Devin's greatest passions were playing and writing music and computer programming. Devin generously shared his love of music and computers by teaching others to learn these skills. As a child Devin spent endless hours 'playing' on a computer, writing code, even building circuit boards. Devin was trained in classical piano and the timpani; he taught himself to play the guitar, drums and many other instruments. As a teen and young adult, he went on to play in numerous Punk Rock bands in the Bay Area and worked as a Computer Engineer. Devin believed that life should be full of beauty, that any project big or small is an opportunity to create a work of art.

With Love and thanks,

Devin's Parents Don and Deborah Devin's Sister Talia

Message from Dr. Solomon

Dear Students,

Devin left us far too soon. He was one of my brightest students and often stayed after class to help others with their work. He was a friend to all who knew him.

I have designed this assignment in collaboration and partnership with the Landes family as a tribute to Devin. My message to each of you is that I want all of my students to take care of yourselves first. Please be mindful of your health.

If you are in need of help or support, please take advantage of the resources SJCC has put in place.

School is important — but it is not more important than you.

Wishing you the best,
Gabriel Solomon, PhD
